# ChalkPicks Competitive Gap Analysis

Research date: 2026-11-08. This is a feature-positioning review based on publicly available official product pages, not a hands-on benchmark of every paid tier.

## Competitive snapshot

| Product | Strongest visible strengths | What ChalkPicks should learn |
|---|---|---|
| OddsJam | Positive-EV feed, arbitrage, middles, low-hold tools, promo conversion, prediction-market coverage, broad odds coverage | Build a fast, unified opportunity feed; clearly show the best price, fair price, market depth/availability, and net EV after fees. |
| Outlier | Deep player-prop research, injury and matchup context, line movement, public sentiment, hit-rate filters, AI analysis, live market workflows | Make player research much deeper: recent and season hit rates, opponent context, role/minutes, injury status, sample size, and both sides of the case. |
| Unabated | Odds screen across books/exchanges/prediction markets, proprietary no-vig consensus, partial-game markets, live/futures views, inline price tools | Prioritize a clean market screen, transparent fair-price methodology, line history, and live/partial-market coverage. |
| Pikkit | Automatic sportsbook bet syncing, 30+ supported books, verified performance tracking, social sharing, line shopping, CLV and portfolio analytics | Reduce manual tracking friction and make the user's own performance a sticky product benefit. |

## ChalkPicks advantages to emphasize

- One destination for sports picks, +EV, arbitrage, Kalshi/prediction markets, CLV, player props, bankroll tools, and backtesting.
- Free calculators can be used as search acquisition pages.
- The product can differentiate through transparent model probabilities, pick timestamps, immutable odds snapshots, and honest backtesting.
- A single Elite subscription can simplify the value proposition if all premium analytics are truly included without credits.

## Highest-priority gaps

### P0 — Trust and data integrity
1. Store an immutable snapshot for every published pick: market, line, odds, sportsbook, model probability, market probability, estimated edge, source timestamp, and model/version.
2. Keep all settled records visible in the archive; never delete losses or silently edit an entry after publication.
3. Publish ROI using a defined stake convention and show sample size, odds assumptions, pushes/voids, and closing-line methodology.
4. Add freshness/availability indicators to every odds-driven tool; stale or unavailable data must never look live.

### P1 — Player-prop research
1. Add L5/L10/L20/season hit-rate views with sample counts and actual game logs.
2. Include opponent rank by relevant stat, minutes/usage/role, injury/availability, and expected game context.
3. Show median and distribution, not just average; flag tiny samples and changed roles.
4. Present counter-evidence and a clear explanation of what would invalidate a projection.

### P1 — Market screen and EV quality
1. Put upcoming and live games into one searchable market board.
2. Compare best available odds to a transparent no-vig consensus, with source book count and update time.
3. Account for limits, liquidity, exchange fees, settlement differences, and stale prices before labeling an opportunity.
4. Show line history and price movement alongside the current opportunity.
5. Keep model probability, market-implied probability, and estimated edge separate in the UI.

### P1 — User retention
1. Add easy manual bet entry first; consider read-only sportsbook syncing only after provider/security/compliance review.
2. Let users follow teams, players, sports, and markets.
3. Provide opt-in alerts for saved filters and meaningful price changes.
4. Make a private portfolio dashboard with ROI by sport, market, odds range, and month.

### P2 — Product polish and distribution
1. Use one searchable tool hub with clear categories and consistent page layouts.
2. Provide glossary/tooltips for implied probability, no-vig, EV, CLV, Kelly, and arbitrage.
3. Create focused SEO pages for real user questions and tools, not thin programmatic pages.
4. Turn methodology and verifiable results into the core conversion proof.
5. Build creator/referral partnerships with tracked links and honest disclosures.

## Positioning recommendation

Do not position ChalkPicks as “the site with the most picks” or promise winning. Position it as:

**The transparent sports-market research workspace: compare prices, understand probability, inspect the evidence, track the result, and test the process.**

That positioning is credible, differentiates the product from picks touts, and makes the broad tool suite easier to understand.
