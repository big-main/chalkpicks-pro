# ChalkPicks Growth Plan

## Goal

Increase qualified traffic and convert more first-time visitors into activated, retained paid members. Optimize for paid conversion and retained value—not raw page views.

## 1. Acquisition funnel

1. **Search discovery:** publish useful pages for high-intent searches such as odds conversion, no-vig probability, expected value, line shopping, bankroll sizing, player props, and sport-specific market analysis.
2. **Free utility:** make the calculators fast, indexable, and genuinely useful without requiring registration.
3. **Proof:** show a transparent, timestamped record of every official pick, including closing price, odds at publication, grading source, pushes/voids, and the complete denominator behind every rate.
4. **Activation:** offer a free account to save filters, follow sports/teams, and receive a limited daily digest.
5. **Conversion:** show the exact Elite feature set, sample analysis, current plan price, cancellation terms, and a clear upgrade path at moments of demonstrated intent.
6. **Retention:** send opt-in alerts for followed sports, price movement, and new picks; summarize each member's tracked performance.

## 2. SEO priorities

- Pick one canonical public domain and enforce it consistently across redirects, canonical tags, Open Graph, JSON-LD, sitemap, email, social profiles, and checkout return URLs.
- Audit Search Console coverage, crawl errors, canonical selection, sitemap processing, and Core Web Vitals weekly.
- Build high-quality sport and market hubs that link to useful tools and current, genuinely updated analysis.
- Give every calculator a unique title, description, explanation, worked example, FAQ, and contextual links to related tools.
- Avoid thin pages generated only by swapping team names. Publish a team/player page only when it contains useful current data, a clear update timestamp, and enough unique analysis to satisfy search intent.
- Keep historical results crawlable on the Backtesting/results archive with transparent filters and source notes.

## 3. Conversion improvements

- Replace generic upgrade prompts with feature-specific previews and explain exactly what Elite unlocks.
- Keep a persistent but unobtrusive upgrade CTA on free pages.
- Show a short product tour: find a market, compare the price to fair probability, inspect the edge, and save/track the pick.
- Add a sample report that is clearly labeled as an example rather than a live pick.
- Test one change at a time: pricing presentation, CTA wording, onboarding length, and the timing of upgrade prompts.
- Track checkout starts, completed payments, cancellations, refunds, and 30-day retention.

## 4. Content and community distribution

- Publish one daily slate recap and one educational post that explains a market concept with sourced data.
- Turn each official pick into a consistent social card with timestamp, market, odds, probability, edge, and a link to its transparent result page.
- Create short vertical videos demonstrating one tool or one market concept; lead with the user problem, not unsupported win-rate claims.
- Use Discord for discussion, onboarding, and feedback. Pin methodology, responsible-use rules, and links to current picks and the results archive.
- Participate in sports and betting communities only where self-promotion is allowed; contribute analysis first and disclose the ChalkPicks affiliation.
- Build partnerships with sports creators and analytics newsletters using tracked referral links and clear disclosure.

## 5. Measurement

Instrument these events with a consistent anonymous/session identifier and user ID after login:

- `landing_view`, `tool_open`, `tool_complete`, `pick_detail_open`
- `signup_started`, `signup_completed`, `onboarding_completed`
- `pricing_view`, `checkout_started`, `subscription_activated`
- `discord_click`, `referral_signup`, `subscription_cancelled`

Review weekly:
- Organic clicks, impressions, indexed pages, and branded vs non-branded traffic
- Visitor-to-signup and signup-to-activated-user conversion
- Pricing-view-to-checkout and checkout-to-paid conversion
- Paid acquisition cost, revenue per member, churn, refunds, and cohort retention
- Tool usage and the features most associated with paid conversion

## 6. Trust and compliance

- Never claim guaranteed profits, guaranteed arbitrage, a fixed win rate, or that a pick is certain.
- Clearly distinguish model probability, market-implied probability, estimated edge, and actual settled outcomes.
- Preserve immutable pick snapshots at publication time so odds and analysis cannot be silently rewritten after the event.
- Display timestamps, odds source, last-updated time, sample size, and grading methodology.
- Include applicable age, location, and responsible-gambling requirements in the product and marketing workflow.

## 7. First 30 days

**Week 1:** verify canonical domain, Search Console, analytics events, broken links, page speed, pricing consistency, and official pick ledger.

**Week 2:** publish improved high-intent calculator pages and one methodology/results explainer; improve signup and pricing flows.

**Week 3:** launch a repeatable daily social-card/video workflow and Discord onboarding; test tracked creator referrals.

**Week 4:** review conversion by channel and landing page, remove low-performing acquisition spend, and double down on channels that produce retained paid members.

## Success criteria

The north-star metric is **new paid members who remain subscribed after 30 days**. Traffic, impressions, signups, and checkout starts are leading indicators—not the final goal.
